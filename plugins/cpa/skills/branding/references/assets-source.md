# Logo Assets

Official logo files for use in CPA deliverables. Inserted programmatically into
PPTX slides, DOCX headers, and XLSX cover sheets by the jhm-brand skill.

## Entity Context
The Clinical Practice Association (CPA) exists within **Johns Hopkins University**
(School of Medicine), not the Johns Hopkins Medicine health system. Therefore:
- **Primary mark**: Johns Hopkins School of Medicine (SOM) logo — `som-logo.png`
- **Secondary/alternative**: JHM enterprise mark — use when the audience or context
  is health-system-facing (e.g., JHH, JHBMC, or enterprise-wide initiatives)

## Files in This Folder

| Filename | Entity | Configuration | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `som-logo.png` | Johns Hopkins School of Medicine | Vertical, navy monochrome | **Primary — white/light backgrounds** |
| `campus-bg.png` | Johns Hopkins campus photograph | Landscape, color | **Cover and closing slides** — faded/ghosted at 10–25% opacity on navy background; right-aligned or full-bleed; atmospheric only, must not compete with text |
| `jhm-logo.png` | Johns Hopkins Medicine | Horizontal, full color | Health-system-facing deliverables (add when available) |
| `jhm-logo-white.png` | Johns Hopkins Medicine | Horizontal, white/reversed | Navy backgrounds, health-system context (add when available) |

## How to Obtain Additional Logo Files
- SOM: https://brand.hopkinsmedicine.org/downloads/our-logo/the-johns-hopkins-school-of-medicine
- JHM: https://brand.hopkinsmedicine.org/downloads/our-logo/johns-hopkins-medicine-logo

## Until Optional Files Are Added
When a logo PNG is not present, the skill will insert a clearly labeled placeholder
at the correct position. Never substitute a font-based or hand-drawn reconstruction.
